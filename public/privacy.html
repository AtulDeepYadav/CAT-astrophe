🐱 Cat-astrophe — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Cat-astrophe is a simple merge game. This policy is short because the app does very little with data.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us or to any server. Cat-astrophe has no account system, no login, and no backend — it doesn't know who you are and has no way to contact you.

What's stored, and where

Everything the game remembers about your progress is saved locally, in your browser or device's own storage (localStorage), and never leaves your device:

  • Your best score and lifetime stats
  • Which cats you've discovered (the Collection Book)
  • Achievements and cosmetic unlocks
  • Your sound/mute preference
  • The local leaderboard and Daily Challenge history
  • A local log of the last 20 crashes/errors, kept only on your device to help diagnose problems if you report one to us — see "Crash logs" below

Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser's site data for this app, permanently deletes all of it. We can't recover it — there's a "Backup" option in the game's menu that lets you copy your own progress out as a portable code if you'd like a copy before doing either.

Crash logs

If the game encounters an error, a short technical note (what went wrong, roughly when) is kept on your device only. It is not automatically sent anywhere. We may ask you to share it with us directly if you report a bug, but that's a choice you make each time — nothing is transmitted in the background.

Ads and analytics — web version

If you're playing at cat-astrophe-rouge.vercel.app or another website, this section is simple: none. No advertising SDKs, no analytics or tracking libraries, no third-party scripts that collect data about you. Everything above this point in the policy describes that version fully.

Ads and analytics — Android app version

The Android app (available on Google Play) is supported by optional ads and an optional one-time purchase, using two third-party services:

  • Google AdMob shows a short rewarded video (only if you choose to watch one, for a free in-game bonus) and an occasional interstitial ad between runs. AdMob may use an advertising identifier and general device/ad-interaction data to select and measure ads — see Google's ad policy for what it collects. We don't receive or store any of that data ourselves.
  • RevenueCat processes the optional "Remove Ads" purchase through Google Play Billing and tells the app whether you've bought it. It sees your purchase receipt and a random, app-specific identifier — never your name, email, or payment details, which Google Play handles directly. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Buying Remove Ads switches both of these off for that install going forward. Everything else in this policy (local-only progress, no account, no backend of our own) is unchanged and applies to the Android app too.

Children's privacy

The web version collects no personal information from anyone, so it applies equally to players of any age. The Android app's ad behavior instead follows whatever audience settings we've declared for it in Google Play Console (visible on the app's Play Store listing) — check there for the current setting if this matters to you.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example, if we add an optional cloud save that requires an account — we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above before that feature ships.
